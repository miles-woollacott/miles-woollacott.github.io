Projects

Merchandising Discounting Strategies and Impact on Revenue | Spring 2026
Completed as part of a Statistical Consulting course, in collaboration with the Carolina Hurricanes National Hockey League team. Engineered pricing elasticity models on 100,000+ transaction records using elastic net regression with autoregressive terms, isolating key promotional levers to optimize discounting strategy and revenue lift.
March Madness Predictor | Spring 2026
Completed as an independent predictive analytics project for the 2026 NCAA Men’s Basketball Tournament. Scraped game-level ESPN data to engineer leave-one-out season metrics in Python, then fit a cross-validated elastic net logistic regression model with team random effects in R to rank competing teams. Correctly predicted 2 of the Final Four participants and accurately forecast several major first-round upsets.
A Primer on Projection Properties of Designs in Computer Experiments | Fall 2025
Completed for PhD requirements at North Carolina State University. Synthesized literature on projection-based computer experimental designs and executed a large-scale simulation study on NCSU High-Performance Computing (HPC) clusters. Evaluated variable selection using spike-and-slab priors on anisotropic lengthscale hyperparameters, demonstrating that standard designs match specialized projection designs and revealing critical tradeoffs between projection properties and classification stability.
Random Projection-Based Testing in High Dimensions | Fall 2024
Completed in a two-person collaboration for an Advanced Statistical Inference course. Evaluated the extension of a novel high-dimensional random projection test statistic (Liu & Huang, 2024) to Poisson distribution settings, running simulations across varying signal strengths and sparsity regimes. Confirmed that the novel test statistic maintains robust power and Type I error control under Poisson specifications, matching original benchmark literature.
